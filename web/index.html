Help and setup

Camera not showing up?

On macOS and Android it should just work. Windows and Linux need a one-time setup:

Linux
  1. Download ix1-linux-setup.sh.
  2. Run sh ~/Downloads/ix1-linux-setup.sh and enter your password. It lets your user account access the camera.
  3. Replug the camera and press Connect camera.
macOS

Use Chrome or Edge. Plug in the camera and press Connect camera.

Windows
  1. Download IX1-Windows-Setup.cmd and double-click it. If Windows warns that it's from the internet, choose More info → Run anyway.
  2. Click Yes when it asks for permission. It attaches Windows' built-in WinUSB driver to the camera.
  3. Come back here and press Connect camera.
Android

Use Chrome. Plug the camera into the phone's USB-C port, press Connect camera and allow access. If another app (such as TI Studio) offers to open the camera, dismiss it.

Recording video

Tap the video button, then the red shutter to start and stop. In Chrome or Edge on a computer you choose where the file goes before recording starts, and it's written as it records. Pick a RAM-backed folder such as /tmp on Fedora to keep it off your SSD (copy it somewhere permanent afterwards). On Android the video downloads when you stop.

Photos and videos

Saved photos and videos are 1024×768 (768×1024 when turned, twice as wide in side by side), whatever the size of the window, with one video frame for each frame from the camera. Markers under Export chooses whether the readings and measurements are drawn on them. Photos also carry the camera's raw temperatures and the view settings inside the PNG, so they can be measured again later; image viewers ignore this, but chat apps and social media usually remove it.

Camera modes

IR shows the thermal picture, Visible the ordinary one, and the rest combine them: PIP (drag the small picture to move it, drag its corner or scroll to resize, ⇄ swaps the two), Fusion (blended; the slider sets how much of the thermal picture shows), IMIX (the thermal colours with the visible picture's texture worked into them, so surfaces, text and edges show while the colours stay thermal; the slider sets how much), Side by side, and Slider (drag the line to wipe between them). The IX1 Go Pro uses its own visible camera; other models use this phone's or computer's camera, which you can also pick on a Go Pro.

If the pictures don't line up, press Align and drag the visible picture into place. Pinch or scroll to zoom it; on a keyboard, the arrow keys nudge it and + / − zoom. The camera lines up better at the distance you aligned it at.

Measuring

Open Measure, pick Point, Line, Frame or Circle, then tap or drag on the image. Tap a measurement to select it: drag to move it, drag its handles to resize, or press × to remove it. Diff shows the difference between two readings.

Shutter calibration

The aperture button at the top closes the camera's shutter for a moment (you'll hear it click) and recalibrates the sensor against it. Press it if the picture looks streaky or blotchy, or after the camera has warmed up or moved somewhere much hotter or colder.

Brightness, contrast and detail

Under Image, tap Brightness or Contrast to show its slider over the picture; tap it again to hide the slider. Brightness slides the picture along the palette and contrast stretches it. Detail switches on and off with each tap and draws outlines from the visible camera onto the thermal picture. Reset all puts the flip, rotation, brightness, contrast and detail back to how they started. None of these change the temperatures.
